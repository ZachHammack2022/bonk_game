Privacy policy

bonk, a small online physics game played in the browser. Last updated 4 November 2026.

This page explains what information bonk collects, how it's used, who it's shared with, how long it's kept, and how you can delete it. You can play bonk without an account; signing in with Google is optional.

1. Playing as a guest (no account)

  • Your player name, ball colour and pattern, settings, stats and recent matches are stored only in your own browser (its local storage). The server does not keep them.
  • While you're in a game room, the server holds your player name and a random identifier for your browser, so it can put you back in your seat if you reconnect. Rooms and this information are deleted from the server's memory shortly after everyone leaves.

2. Signing in with Google

If you choose Sign in with Google, bonk asks Google only for basic profile information (the openid, email and profile scopes). From Google, bonk receives and stores:

  • your Google account ID (a number that identifies your Google account to bonk),
  • your email address (if Google says it's verified), and
  • your first name.

bonk never receives your Google password, and does not access your contacts, files, email messages, calendar or any other Google data.

How this information is used: only to sign you in and keep your bonk profile (player name, ball, settings, stats and your last 50 matches) the same on every device you sign in on. Your email address is shown only to you (on the home page, as the account you're signed in with); other players see only the player name you choose. bonk does not use your information for advertising, does not sell it, and does not use it to build profiles of you beyond your game stats.

Sharing: bonk does not share, sell or transfer information received from Google to anyone. It is stored on the server that runs bonk, hosted by Fly.io, which stores it on bonk's behalf and has no right to use it for anything else.

bonk's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

3. Cookies

  • If you sign in, a cookie keeps you signed in for up to 90 days. It contains only a random code; the server stores a scrambled (hashed) copy, not the code itself. It's removed when you sign out.
  • While you sign in, a second cookie holds a random check code for up to 10 minutes, to protect the sign-in from forgery.
  • bonk uses no advertising or tracking cookies, and no third-party analytics.

4. Game and technical information

  • Playtest reports: after a match your browser sends a short report: ping, frame rate, how smooth the game was, your device type (for example "phone, Chrome") and, if you answer, how the match felt. It includes your player name but not your email or IP address. The server keeps the latest 500 reports in memory; they're cleared when the server restarts. JavaScript errors are reported the same way.
  • Reports: if someone reports a player, the server keeps the report: both players' names (and account IDs if signed in), the reason and note, and what happened in that room in the last few minutes (who joined or left, name changes, emotes, removals). The latest 500 are kept in memory and in the logs, for whoever runs the server to review.
  • Logs: the server logs events such as rooms opening and matches ending, with room codes and account IDs, but not email addresses. Player names are logged only for moderation: reports, someone removed from a room, and names the filter replaced.
  • IP addresses are used while you're connected, to limit abuse (for example, how many rooms one network can make). They aren't stored.

5. How long information is kept

  • Account information and your profile are kept until you delete your account.
  • Sign-in sessions end after 90 days, or when you sign out.
  • The hosting provider keeps daily backups of the server's disk for up to 30 days; deleted accounts disappear from those as they expire.

6. Your choices: access, export and deletion

  • See and save your data: signed in, open Your stats on the home page and choose Download my data. As a guest, your profile is only in your browser (clearing the site's data removes it).
  • Delete your account: open Your stats and choose Delete my account. Your account, profile and sign-ins are deleted from the server immediately.
  • Remove bonk's access to your Google account: at any time from your Google Account's third-party connections page.
  • Sign out on the home page. Signing out doesn't delete anything.

7. Children

bonk is not directed at children under 13, and doesn't knowingly collect information from them. Anyone can play as a guest without giving any information.
