Who comes back

days are UTC

Of the players who first joined a game on a day, how many joined one again 1, 7 and 30 days later (on exactly that day). Rough targets for a casual game: D1 above 35%, D7 above 10%. Each browser counts once; clearing its storage makes it a new player.

Where players are

Where each connection comes in (the Fly.io region that took it, roughly where the player is) and how laggy it is: the round trip each player's browser measures. Typical is the median; slow is the worst 5%. Over 150 ms, people start to feel it. "direct" means not through Fly's proxy (running locally).

And in matches: the drawing delay is how far behind the newest states the match is drawn, which the browser grows when states come unevenly (roughly 40–80 ms on a steady connection). A stall is a state coming more than 100 ms after the one before: on a WebSocket, one lost packet holds up everything behind it. Stalling often is the share of players in a match who stalled more than once a minute. Lots of stalls, or a drawing delay well above 80 ms, is the case for sending states over UDP (WebTransport); few is the case against.
